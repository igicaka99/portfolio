/ What I do

Services

A brand is more than a logo. I start by studying the business, its market and competitors, and only then define the idea the identity stands on. From there come the logo, typography, colour, graphic elements and the rules that keep everything consistent — from a business card to a façade.

Research · Concept · Logo · Type & colour · Brand guidelines

Packaging has a few seconds on the shelf to be noticed and understood. I study the category and the competition, put the information in order of importance, then design the label, the box and the dieline — checked for print, material and real-life use.

Shelf analysis · Concept · Label & box · Dieline · Print-ready files

Long publications need a clear system to read easily page after page. I plan the structure, grid and typography first, then lay out every spread and prepare the files for print.

Structure · Grid & typography · Layout · Image editing · Prepress

Posters, flyers, roll-ups and ads that are understood at first glance. One clear message, the right format for where it will be seen, and files checked to the last detail before they reach the printer.

Message · Layout · Formats & adaptations · Print-ready files

A façade is read from across the street, often in passing. I work from photos and measurements of the real site, show a realistic visualisation before anything is produced, and prepare the files for the sign maker.

Site survey · Visualisation · Signs & window graphics · Production files

AI is a tool in my process, not a shortcut. I use it to explore ideas and create product visuals faster — but the concept, the composition, every correction and the final quality control stay in my hands.

Art direction · Product visuals · Retouching · Quality control
/ How I work

Every project follows the same four steps — so you always know where we are and what comes next.

01

Research

Before the first sketch, I get to know the business: who it is for, what the competition does and what needs to change.

02

Concept

Together we settle on one clear idea and direction. Everything that follows grows from it.

03

Design

I develop the solution in detail and we refine it together, based on your feedback.

04

Delivery

Print-ready and digital files, checked to the last detail — with guidance on how to use them.

Igor Petkov

Graphic designer · Pleven, Bulgaria

I have been designing since 2019 and working professionally since 2022 — over these years many brands have passed through my hands, from local shops to established companies.

I take a project from the first idea to the finished file: the concept, the logo and identity, packaging, catalogues and print, signage and social media — down to print-ready artwork checked to the last detail. The aim is always the same: a design that fits the business and works in the real world.

AI is a big part of how I work — to explore ideas, build product visuals and move faster. The concept and the decisions stay mine. I keep learning and raising the bar with every project.
