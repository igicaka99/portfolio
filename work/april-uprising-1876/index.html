April Uprising 1876

Collector’s edition · Wall calendar · Print

Each year DOM-BG publishes a calendar devoted to the Bulgarian National Revival — not a promotional giveaway, but a collector’s edition. The 2026 issue marks 150 years since the April Uprising. Every sheet is composed like a museum exhibit: an archival portrait or a real period painting, the words of a revolutionary on aged paper, a condensed display serif and the client’s deep green. A calendar meant to be kept long after its year is over.
