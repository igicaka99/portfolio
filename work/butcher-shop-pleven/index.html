Butcher Shop Pleven

Shopfront graphics · Illuminated signage

Facade design for a boutique butcher shop in Pleven — a real store, shown as a visualisation refined with AI, the way I present every shopfront project before it goes to print. Window graphics, illuminated letters and two round lightboxes work as one system with a rugged, grill-house character: charcoal surfaces, torn craft paper, bold slab-serif headlines and close-up meat photography. The message stays simple and local — fresh Bulgarian meat.

Built to be read from across the street

Two facades, one system

The front carries the main message — fresh Bulgarian meat — on one continuous glass surface, door included. Above it, the illuminated letters and round lightboxes keep the shop visible after dark. The side facade adds three smaller panels in the same torn-paper, slab-serif language.
